Part 1 · Your hardest partIs → it. Are → they.

Look at the first word of the question. The answer uses the same word.
Is the lion…? → Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.
Are the zebras…? → Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.

Part 2 · New fridgeThere is or there are?

is / isn't → milk, juice, cheese, bread, rice, chicken (we can't count them) and one thing.
are / aren't → words with -s: eggs, grapes, tomatoes, sausages.
Trick: look at the end of the word. -s → are.

Part 3a, some or any?

Ask yourself two questions:
1. Is it one thing? (a pear, a mango) → a
2. No? Is the sentence yes or no? Yes → some. No (isn't / aren't) → any.
